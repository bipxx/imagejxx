  tests ending TCZ equality; a headless away/back position round trip alone
  creates no new epoch. Owner away/back stales through activation identity.

  Pointer publication additionally requires the captured Display revision and
  XY transform. Never recapture on release to bypass a stale guard.

  Pixels/Metadata revisions, shape/type/policy, row count and complete original
  row. Original Selection need not match; fresh captured Data guards publication.
  Capture fresh Selection/activation/TCZ. Historical
  selection revision/position remain provenance. Replay may target the bound
  row from another current TCZ; do not reuse interactive visibility admission
  unchanged. Reopened owners require an explicit new row plus complete
  replacement for rebind.

  identity/constraints. Changed acceptance advances Metadata and Selection
  once, creates one undo item and clears redo; Pixels/Display/global selections
  follow D02 unchanged. No-op creates no history, dirty or workflow change.

  before preparation callbacks or large copies. Control/progress/UI callbacks
  stay outside publication locks; existing nonreentrant constraint validators
  retain clarification003's exception. Use D03's checked logical working bound,
